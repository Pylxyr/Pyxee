  Source code and licence: https://ffmpeg.org/ and https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later. You may replace the bundled ffmpeg.exe with
  your own build: put it next to Pyxee as ffmpeg.exe, or set the PYXEE_FFMPEG environment variable.
